Build 14 still presented a Take Photo path on iPad. Build 15 does not.

Please test: sign in → My T / Profile → Choose profile photo. You will get
the photo library, not Take Photo.

The app does not need persistent background location. GPS is when-in-use
while the app is open (dispatch / maps). The archive copies Info.plist with
UIBackgroundModes = remote-notification only. "location" is stripped if any
plugin tries to add it.

--- Guideline 3.1.1 account registration ---
The iOS app no longer includes account registration. It is sign-in only for
people whose contracting business already has a Tradesman workspace.
New business accounts are created on https://www.tradesman-us.com in Safari,
not inside the App Store app.

--- Guidelines 3.1.1 / 3.1.3(c) In-App Purchase ---
Tradesman is sold to contracting businesses and their employees, not to
consumers or families. There is no family plan and no consumer SKU.

On iPhone and iPad we do not collect subscription payment and we do not
register new accounts. Plan information opens in Safari (United States
storefront). “Collect from customers” is the contractor billing their job
customers for trade work — it is not a sale of Tradesman subscriptions.

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the detailed feedback on submission bfc9a7fb-95be-473b-a1d4-5009b425ae82
(version 1.0, build 13) and for the crash logs. We have addressed each item in a new
binary (2.3.18, build 14).

--- Guideline 2.3.10 (screenshots) ---
We replaced the screenshots. The previous set incorrectly included non-iOS status bars.
The new screenshots were captured from iOS / iPadOS and show the Tradesman app in use
(dashboard, conversations, estimates, and calendar).

--- Guideline 2.1(a) microphone ---
Build 12 did not reliably request microphone permission, so the control appeared
unresponsive on iPad. The new build includes NSMicrophoneUsageDescription, requests
microphone access when the user taps a mic control, and shows an on-screen message
if access is denied or unavailable. Please test: My T → voicemail greeting → Record,
or the on-screen assistant microphone after allowing the system prompt.

--- Guideline 2.1(a) camera crash ---
The crash logs show a TCC abort: build 13 (2.3.14) opened UIImagePickerController
without NSCameraUsageDescription in the shipped Info.plist. The new binary includes
that key (and microphone / photo library strings). Profile photo on iPad now uses a
native picker (Take Photo / Photo Library) with permission handling instead of the
WKWebView file input that killed the previous build.
Please test: My T → Contact & profile → Upload profile photo → Take Photo.

--- Guideline 5.1.1(v) account deletion ---
Account deletion is completed inside the app. No email, extra account, or support
ticket is required:

1. Sign in with the demo account (credentials are in App Review Information).
2. Open My T (Account / Profile).
3. Under Contact & profile, use the Delete account box (also under System and
   Mobile Settings, and a red button next to Save).
4. Type DELETE, tap Delete my account, and confirm.

This permanently deletes the login. Please do not delete the demo account; we can
provide a throwaway user if you need to run the button. Public instructions (same
in-app steps, no email requirement): https://www.tradesman-us.com/account-deletion

--- Guideline 3.1.1 / 3.1.3(c) In-App Purchase ---
Tradesman is a B2B field-service workspace sold to contracting businesses (including
sole proprietors operating as a business), not to consumers or families. Seats are
for that company’s workers.

On the iOS app we do not collect subscription payment. Plan checkout and Autopay
open in Safari on https://www.tradesman-us.com (United States storefront link-out).
The app is a client for work the business already purchased on the web. Customer
“Collect from customers” is the contractor billing their job customers for trade
work — it is not a sale of Tradesman digital subscriptions.

--- Guideline 2.5.4 background location ---
The new binary does not declare location in UIBackgroundModes. Location is
when-in-use only (dispatch radius / maps while the app is open). We do not
need persistent background location.

--- Guideline 2.1(b) business model ---

1. Who uses the paid subscriptions and features?
   Licensed trade contractors and their employees (roofing, remodeling, and similar
   field-service businesses). These are business users, not consumers or families.

2. Where do users purchase subscriptions and features?
   On our website during signup and billing: https://www.tradesman-us.com
   Payment is collected by Helcim as our merchant processor. Users do not purchase
   subscriptions through the App Store or In-App Purchase.

3. What previously purchased subscriptions can a user access in the app?
   A monthly business SaaS plan purchased on the website (for example Base,
   Office Manager tiers, Corporate, or a single-tool plan). After they sign in,
   the iOS app opens the same workspace they already paid for on the web.

4. What paid content is unlocked in the app without In-App Purchase?
   The business workspace: customers, estimates, calendar, conversations (SMS/email),
   and related field-service tools. Access is unlocked by the website subscription
   already associated with that login. No digital goods are sold inside the iOS app.

5. Are the enterprise services sold to single users, consumers, or for family use?
   Sold to businesses (the contracting company). Seats are for that company’s
   employees. Not sold to consumers and not a family plan.

This is a multi-platform / enterprise business tool. The iOS app is a client for
work the company already purchased on the web. We are not offering consumer
subscriptions or unlocking paid digital content through the iOS app.

Please use the demo account in App Review Information to sign in. Thank you.
