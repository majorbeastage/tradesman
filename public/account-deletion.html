Delete your Tradesman account

You can delete your account from inside the Tradesman app. This page is the fallback if you cannot sign in.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open My T (Account).
  3. Open Delete account under System and Mobile Settings.
  4. Type DELETE, then tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion closes your login and removes personal profile data. Some business records may be retained where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or carrier rules.

If you cannot sign in

  1. Email admin@tradesman-us.com from the same email address you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject line “Tradesman — delete my account”, or open this pre-filled deletion request.
