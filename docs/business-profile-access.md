# Business profile access — how to invite Tradesman

Save each public profile URL in **Growth → Business profiles**, then invite Tradesman as a **Manager / Partner** (you keep Ownership). Prefer invite-based access — never send passwords in chat or email.

**Invite email:** use the address your Tradesman contact gives you, or `admin@tradesman-us.com`.

## Quick checklist

1. Add the public URL for every profile you use.
2. Invite Tradesman with Manager (not Owner) access where possible.
3. Tell us when the invite is sent so we can accept it the same day.
4. After access is accepted, we can post, reply, and run ads under your approval and campaign budget rules.

---

## Website

**Why:** Live URL for landing pages, tracking, and grading. CMS login only if we should publish pages.

1. Paste your homepage URL in Growth (https://…).
2. If we will edit the site: open your host’s Users / Team settings (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, GoDaddy, etc.).
3. Invite `admin@tradesman-us.com` (or your Tradesman contact) as **Editor** — not Owner unless you intend full ownership.
4. Tell us which pages we may change.

## Google Business Profile

**Why:** Maps, local search, posts, reviews, and (later) Reserve with Google.

1. Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) as Owner.
2. Select your location → **Users** (People and access).
3. Add Tradesman’s email as **Managers**.
4. Paste your Maps / g.page link in Growth and check “I manage this Google Business listing” when you control it.
5. Complete any Google verification prompts as Owner.

Do **not** transfer Ownership unless you explicitly want Tradesman to own the listing.

## Facebook Page

**Why:** Page posts, Meta ads, Messenger, and Instagram linking.

1. Open Meta Business Suite or Page → Settings → **Page access**.
2. Invite Tradesman with full control or content + ads permissions.
3. Or in Business Manager: **Partners → Add partner** and share the Page (and Ad Account for ads).
4. Confirm the invite, then paste your `facebook.com/…` URL in Growth.

## Instagram

**Why:** Creative, Reels, and Meta ads with Instagram placements.

1. Use a Professional (Business/Creator) account.
2. Link Instagram to your Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite.
3. Assign Tradesman the same people/partner access as the Page.
4. Paste your `instagram.com/…` URL in Growth.

Link Instagram to the Facebook Page before inviting us — otherwise ads and scheduling are much harder.

## LinkedIn Company Page

**Why:** B2B presence and LinkedIn ads.

1. Company Page → Admin tools → **Manage admins**.
2. Invite Tradesman as Content admin (or Sponsored Content poster for ads).
3. In Campaign Manager, grant the same person ad account access.
4. Paste your `linkedin.com/company/…` URL in Growth.

## Yelp

**Why:** Local discovery and reviews.

1. Sign in to Yelp for Business.
2. Account → Users → invite Tradesman with manager permissions.
3. Paste your Yelp biz URL in Growth.

Paid Yelp advertising is separate from the free listing.

## TikTok

**Why:** Short-form video and TikTok Ads.

1. Use a TikTok Business account.
2. Business Center / Manage permissions → invite Tradesman as Analyst or Advertiser (higher only if posting).
3. Share the Ad Account for paid media.
4. Paste your `tiktok.com/@…` URL in Growth.

## X (Twitter)

**Why:** Brand presence and optional X Ads.

1. Settings → Security and account access → **Delegate** / Teams.
2. Invite Tradesman as Admin or Contributor.
3. For ads: X Ads → Account access → invite with Ad manager rights.
4. Paste your `x.com/…` URL in Growth.

If Delegate/Teams is unavailable, create invite-based access another way — avoid sharing the main password when possible.
